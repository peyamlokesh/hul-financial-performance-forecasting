# Financial Performance & Forecasting of Hindustan Unilever Limited

## 1. Introduction

Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) is a major Indian fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) company with products across home care, beauty and wellbeing, personal care, and foods. Financial performance analysis helps understand the company's growth, profitability and operating efficiency.

This project examines HUL's historical financial performance using published company data and Microsoft Excel. It evaluates turnover, EBITDA, profit after tax, annual growth rates and profitability margins. A simplified forecast is also developed to demonstrate the application of financial planning and analysis techniques.

## 2. Objectives

- To examine HUL's historical turnover and profit performance.
- To calculate annual growth rates for turnover, EBITDA and profit after tax.
- To evaluate EBITDA and PAT margins.
- To calculate compound annual growth rates over the study period.
- To develop an illustrative financial forecast using historical trends.
- To present financial information through an Excel dashboard.

## 3. Data Sources and Methodology

The project uses secondary data from HUL's official financial performance pages and annual reports. The historical study covers FY2015–16 to FY2025–26, and the illustrative forecast covers FY2026–27.

Microsoft Excel is used for data organization, financial calculations, trend analysis, forecasting and dashboard preparation.

The analysis uses annual growth rates, profitability margins, compound annual growth rate (CAGR) and recent historical averages. The forecast assumes that recent growth and profitability trends provide a reasonable starting point for a simplified baseline estimate.

## 4. Financial Performance Analysis

### Turnover Analysis

HUL's reported turnover increased from ₹33,891 crore in FY2015–16 to ₹63,763 crore in FY2025–26. This indicates substantial growth in the company's reported turnover over the study period. However, individual years show different growth rates, so long-term expansion should be considered alongside annual performance.

### EBITDA Analysis

EBITDA increased from ₹5,749 crore in FY2015–16 to ₹15,054 crore in FY2025–26. EBITDA helps evaluate operating earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Analysing EBITDA alongside turnover helps determine whether operating earnings are keeping pace with business growth.

### Profit After Tax Analysis

Reported profit after tax increased from ₹4,137 crore in FY2015–16 to ₹10,652 crore in FY2025–26. PAT reflects the profit remaining after tax and relevant reported items. Changes in PAT margins and annual growth rates provide additional insight into profitability.

### Profitability Margins

EBITDA margin measures EBITDA relative to turnover, while PAT margin measures reported profit after tax relative to turnover. These ratios help assess the company's ability to convert turnover into operating earnings and reported profit.

## 5. Forecasting Methodology

The FY2026–27 forecast uses the average of the three latest annual turnover growth observations. Forecast turnover is calculated by applying this average growth rate to the latest actual turnover.

Forecast EBITDA and PAT are calculated by applying the average of the three latest annual EBITDA and PAT margins, respectively, to forecast turnover.

This method is transparent and easy to reproduce in Excel. However, it assumes that recent historical trends continue and does not separately model changes in product mix, costs, taxation, acquisitions, disposals or exceptional items.

The forecast is an illustrative model estimate and is not official company guidance.

## 6. Key Findings

1. HUL's reported turnover expanded substantially over the historical period.
2. EBITDA and PAT increased over the study period, indicating higher reported earnings in absolute terms.
3. Annual growth rates varied, demonstrating the importance of examining year-on-year performance alongside long-term trends.
4. EBITDA and PAT margins provide additional information about profitability beyond turnover alone.
5. A historical-average forecast provides a useful baseline for financial planning, but actual results may differ from the model.

## 7. Recommendations

- Monitor turnover growth alongside EBITDA and PAT growth.
- Review changes in profitability margins to identify possible cost or earnings pressures.
- Compare actual results with forecasts and investigate material differences.
- Update forecast assumptions when new annual results become available.
- Use management commentary and annual-report disclosures to understand changes that cannot be explained by numerical trends alone.

## 8. Limitations

The analysis is based on published historical data and a simplified forecasting method. Historical reporting definitions, restatements and discontinued operations may affect year-to-year comparisons. The model does not incorporate all factors influencing future financial performance, and its estimates should not be interpreted as guaranteed results.

## 9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyze a company's financial performance and prepare a basic forecast. It combines financial statement data, growth calculations, profitability ratios, CAGR, forecasting formulas and dashboard visualization. The project also demonstrates the importance of using reliable sources, documenting assumptions and interpreting financial results carefully.
